The evaluation of 25 quantized open-source large language models (LLMs) across three single-board computers (SBCs): Raspberry Pi 4, Raspberry Pi 5, and Orange Pi 5 Pro, using two inference runtimes: Ollama and Llamafile. Key findings:

- SBCs can reliably support up to 1.5B parameter models.
- Llamafile achieves up to 4x higher generation throughput than Ollama.
- Llamafile also shows 30–40% lower power consumption compared to Ollama under varying CPU configurations and prompt types.

Performance metrics evaluated include generation throughput, memory usage, and power consumption.
