The research deliverables include:

- A benchmark report comparing generation throughput, memory usage, and power consumption of 25 quantized open-source LLMs on three SBCs: Raspberry Pi 4, Raspberry Pi 5, and Orange Pi 5 Pro.
  
- Detailed results for each model across both Ollama and Llamafile runtimes under varying CPU configurations.

- Insights into the reliability of SBCs in supporting models up to 1.5B parameters.

- Recommendations for optimal setup configurations based on evaluated workloads (e.g., prompt types).

- Production-ready code examples for deploying quantized LLMs on these platforms.

- Analysis of power consumption and cost implications for edge inference.

- Discussion on privacy considerations and security in localized AI deployments.
