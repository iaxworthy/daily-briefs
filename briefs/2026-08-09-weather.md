The San Francisco and surrounding areas have issued a Heat Advisory with daytime highs in the mid-90s to low-100s and nighttime lows expected around 70°F through Sunday evening. Elevated fire weather conditions are expected across the interior North and East Bays, as well as the Diablo and Santa Lucia ranges, through Monday. Coastal areas will see strong winds, with some offshore winds developing tonight into early Monday. Persistent stratus clouds cover much of Monterey Bay, reducing clearing until later in the day. A Heat Advisory remains in effect for the Interior Bay Area and Central Coast through 10 PM PDT Sunday.
