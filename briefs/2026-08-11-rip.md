### Deliverable: 

Evaluate inference performance of 25 quantized open-source LLMs across three popular single-board computers (SBCs): Raspberry Pi 4, Raspberry Pi 5, and Orange Pi 5 Pro using two inference runtimes: Ollama and Llamafile. Measure generation throughput, memory usage, and power consumption under varying CPU configurations with prompt types simulating realistic workloads.

### Key Findings:

- SBCs can reliably support models up to 1.5B parameters.
- Llamafile achieves up to 4x higher throughput compared to Ollama.
- Llamafile also demonstrates 30–40% lower power usage than Ollama across various configurations and prompt types.

### Recommendations:

- For optimal performance, use Llamafile with Raspberry Pi 5 or Orange Pi 5 Pro for models up to 1.5B parameters.
- Explore custom models optimized for these SBCs for even better efficiency.
