### Deliverable: Evaluation of LLM Inference on Popular Single-board Computers

**Title:** An Evaluation of LLMs Inference on Raspberry Pi 4, 5, and Orange Pi 5 Pro

**Key Findings:**
- SBCs can reliably support models up to 1.5B parameters.
- Llamafile achieves up to 4x higher throughput than Ollama.
- Llamafile also reduces power consumption by 30–40% compared to Ollama.

**Methodology:**
- Benchmarked 25 quantized open-source LLMs across three SBCs (Raspberry Pi 4, 5, and Orange Pi 5 Pro).
- Used two inference runtimes: Ollama and Llamafile.
- Evaluated generation throughput, memory usage, and power consumption under varying CPU configurations and prompt types.

**Conclusion:**
SBCs are viable platforms for lightweight LLM deployment. Llamafile demonstrates superior performance in terms of throughput and energy efficiency, suitable for edge AI applications.
