### Deliverable: Evaluation of LLMs Inference on Popular Single-board Computers

#### Abstract:
This work benchmarks the performance of 25 quantized open-source large language models (LLMs) across three single-board computers (SBCs)—Raspberry Pi 4, Raspberry Pi 5, and Orange Pi 5 Pro—using two inference runtimes: Ollama and Llamafile. The evaluation measures generation throughput, memory usage, and power consumption under varying CPU configurations, simulating realistic workloads with multiple prompt types.

#### Key Findings:
1. **Throughput**: SBCs can reliably support models up to 1.5B parameters using lightweight quantized versions.
2. **Memory Usage**: Llamafile achieves higher throughput compared to Ollama for the same models, likely due to optimized resource management.
3. **Power Consumption**: Power usage varies by model and runtime; Orange Pi Pro shows better performance in terms of power efficiency.
4. **Workload Simulations**: Results indicate that prompt type significantly impacts inference speed.

#### Recommendations:
1. For lightweight LLMs (up to 1.5B parameters), SBCs like Raspberry Pi 5 are viable for local inference, provided they have sufficient RAM and CPU cores.
2. Use optimized runtimes such as Llamafile for better performance without compromising power efficiency.
3. Further research is needed on model-specific optimizations and power consumption strategies.
