### Deliverable: Evaluation of LLMs Inference on Popular Single-board Computers

#### Abstract:
We evaluated 25 quantized open-source LLMs across three single-board computers (SBCs): Raspberry Pi 4, Raspberry Pi 5, and Orange Pi 5 Pro using two inference runtimes—Ollama and Llamafile. Results show SBCs can reliably support models up to 1.5B parameters with Llamafile achieving up to 4x higher throughput and 30-40% lower power usage than Ollama under varying CPU configurations.

#### Key Findings:
- **Raspberry Pi 5**: Up to 1.5B parameters, Llamafile outperforms by ~40% in throughput.
- **Orange Pi 5 Pro**: Similar performance as Raspberry Pi 5, Llamafile offers better efficiency.
- **Ollama**: Generally slower and less power-efficient compared to Llamafile.

#### Recommendations:
For models up to 1.5B parameters, use Raspberry Pi 5 or Orange Pi 5 Pro with Llamafile for optimal performance and lower energy consumption. For larger models, consider cloud inference.
