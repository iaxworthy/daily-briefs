### Deliverable: Performance Evaluation of 25 Quantized Open-Source LLMS on Raspberry Pi 4, 5, and Orange Pi 5 Pro

This study benchmarks the performance of 25 quantized open-source LLMs across three single-board computers (SBCs) – Raspberry Pi 4, Raspberry Pi 5, and Orange Pi 5 Pro. Key findings include:

- SBCs can reliably support models up to 1.5B parameters.
- Llamafile runtime achieves better performance than Ollama under varying CPU configurations.
- Generation throughput, memory usage, and power consumption are evaluated using multiple prompt types.

Results suggest Raspberry Pi 4 and 5 are suitable platforms for lightweight LLM inference, with Orange Pi Pro supporting a slightly larger model size.
